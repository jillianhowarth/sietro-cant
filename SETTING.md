# Setting — the underworld of Sietro

Sietro is a wealthy trade republic on the Navinian Coast — medieval Venice with dragons.
Canals, salt, masks, and money; a cunning, hospitable, competitive people where vendettas
simmer and nobles poison each other over patronage. Its wealth comes from its position on the
great trade routes, from fishing, and from a brutal, lucrative salt industry.

This file is the **ground the cant stands on** — the anchor that keeps every coined term
feeling like it belongs to *this* city. The thieves' cant is the street argot of Sietro's
underworld: ordinary words about water, salt, fish, masks, ivy, and crows, reassigned to
secret meanings so a fence and a smuggler can talk business in front of the Watch and sound
like they're gossiping about the weather.

## The law
- **The City Watch** — the main peacekeepers, mostly former navy. They really only guard the
  northern islands (Ivy, Merchant, Naval). In the Fratelli District they're paid to look away.
- **The City Guard** — the common name for the Sietran Navy. Only stirs for the Naval District,
  foreign threats, or major trouble. Captain of the Watch: **Eliseo Bortolo** (big mustache).
- **The gallows** stand on a wooden platform in the **Piazza della Popolo** (Merchant District).
  Public executions weekly, by hanging or beheading. Stocks scattered around for lesser crimes.

## The families and the Collective
- **Vicenza Family** — led by Celso Vicenza. Old, powerful, controls the *western* Fratelli
  District. Hands deep in the Rogati (the elected senate).
- **Renoldi Family** — led by Lorenzo Renoldi. Old Sietran crime, fading fast — losing the
  *eastern* Fratelli to the Collective.
- **The Collective** — foreign, from the mainland to the north, arrived only ~5 years ago.
  Siloed and faceless: even its own members don't know who's above them or what the real goal
  is. It swallowed most of Renoldi's turf. An outsider machine in a city of old family crime.

## The ground that matters
- **Fratelli District** — the underworld's home. Traded its vote for low taxes and no scrutiny.
  Fences, forgers, and smugglers ply their trade openly in its piazzas. West = Vicenza,
  East = the Collective (once Renoldi).
- **The Puddles** — a flood-wracked shantytown on the western edge; no real canals or
  infrastructure, almost always wet. Where the poor, the sick, and the disposed-of end up.
  The Ivy District floods it on purpose by working its sluices.
- **The Western Docks** — every traveler's first sight of Sietro; rough and brawling, the Watch
  turns a blind eye. The gateway for smuggled cargo.
- **The Ivy District** — the nobles, the Duke, the Rogati. Ivy on the towers; poison in the
  gardens. Controls the city's water levels.
- **Piazza Del Scambiatore** (Fratelli) — a market for under-the-table deals, with an extensive
  black market if you know who to ask. The **Withered Bloom**, a failing flower shop, hides an
  entrance to the Collective's underground. The **House of Vornoth** is a no-questions hospital
  whose undertakers make bodies disappear.
- **The Flats** — the salt fields, worked in the summer heat. Lucrative for those at the top,
  backbreaking and exploitative for the harvesters.

## Flavor to keep the cant true
Understated, gallows-humored, deniable. Sietrans are gracious and cunning at once. Dragons are
real but hidden — the Duke himself is secretly a bronze dragon. The cant should sound like
harmless dock-and-market chatter to anyone who doesn't hold the key.
