# Conventions — how the cant is spoken

Starter conventions. The daily keeper adds one new convention every 10 days — keep additions
short and consistent with what's here. (This is the cant's equivalent of a conlang's grammar:
rules of use rather than new vocabulary.)

## Convention 1 — Weather is law
Talk about the weather to talk about the Watch. "High water," "the tide," "fog," and "a dry
night" all describe how much heat is on the street, never the sky.

## Convention 2 — Prices are risks
When someone quotes a price in a deal ("that's a salty ask," "cheap at the Flats"), they're
describing danger and effort, not literal coin. Real numbers are spoken plainly; a cant "price"
is always a metaphor for risk.

## Convention 3 — Never name the Collective
You don't say "the Collective." You say "the north wind," "the quiet ones," or just tip your
head north. Naming them outright marks you as an outsider — or a fool.
