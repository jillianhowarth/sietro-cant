# Core imagery — the source images of the cant

The cant works by reassigning Sietro's ordinary material world to underworld meanings. These
are the governing images every term should descend from. New terms **extend one of these
families** rather than inventing an unrelated picture — that's what keeps the whole cant
sounding like one city's secret. (These are the cant's "roots.") Format: image — established
underworld sense.

## Water & the law
- **tide / high water** — the City Watch, or danger sweeping in ("the tide's up" = Watch about)
- **the sluices** — the nobles or the law deliberately ruining someone (the Ivy floods the Puddles at will)
- **the Puddles** — where bodies and nobodies are dumped; a bad end
- **getting wet** — getting caught, or getting drowned
- **fog** — good cover; conditions ripe for work

## Salt & labor (money)
- **salt** — coin, money, profit
- **the Flats** — a grinding, exploited job or a long con that wears you down
- **brine** — a bribe; money that buys silence

## Docks & fishing (the trade)
- **a catch / a haul** — a successful score
- **cargo** — contraband
- **longshoreman** — a smuggler, a mover of goods
- **the ferry** — a getaway, passage out; or crossing over (death)

## Masks & Carnival (identity)
- **a face / a mask** — a false identity, a disguise
- **a pomander** — a cover story that keeps trouble at arm's length

## Ivy & the gardens (nobility & poison)
- **ivy** — a noble, a patron, creeping influence
- **a sprig / something green** — poison
- **the vine** — a chain of patronage; noble intrigue

## Crows & the gallows (death & informing)
- **a crow / raven** — an informant, or an undertaker who makes bodies vanish (House of Vornoth)
- **the wood / the platform** — the gallows; the price of snitching
- **snuffing a candle** — a killing

## The foreign wind (the Collective)
- **the north wind** — the Collective; faceless outsiders from the mainland
