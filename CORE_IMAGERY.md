# Core imagery — the source images of the cant

The cant works by reassigning Sietro's ordinary material world to underworld meanings. These
are the governing images every term should descend from. New terms **extend one of these
families** rather than inventing an unrelated picture — that's what keeps the whole cant
sounding like one city's secret. (These are the cant's "roots.")

## The seven families
There are exactly seven families. The `extends:` field of a lexicon entry must be one of these
seven names, spelled exactly as the headings below. Each family has a **domain**: the kinds of
hidden meaning it is allowed to carry. A term's hidden meaning must fall inside its family's
domain — sharing a word with the family is not enough. (Noble patronage is ivy; a crime
family's patronage is not, because a crime family is not nobility.)

Each family also has a **vocabulary pool**: the ordinary things Sietrans in that world actually
name every day. A new term is one of those things, said with its plain meaning, that happens to
carry a second one. Established senses are listed as image — underworld sense.

## Water & the law
**Domain:** the Watch, the law, danger closing in, being caught, ruin from above, good cover.
Not for: people of the trade, money, goods.
**Pool:** tides, floods, fog, rain, canals, sluices, the Puddles, wet stone, drowning, the hours
of the tide.
- **tide / high water** — the City Watch, or danger sweeping in ("the tide's up" = Watch about;
  the old Sietran name for a full Watch surge is *acqua alta*)
- **the sluices** — the nobles or the law deliberately ruining someone (the Ivy floods the Puddles at will)
- **the Puddles** — where bodies and nobodies are dumped; a bad end
- **getting wet** — getting caught, or getting drowned
- **fog** — good cover; conditions ripe for work

## Salt & labor
**Domain:** money, payment, debt, bribes, profit, and the grinding work that earns them.
Not for: people, violence, goods.
**Pool:** salt, brine, the Flats, the pans, the rake, the harvest, sacks, curing, the summer heat,
sweat, a day's wage.
- **salt** — coin, money, profit
- **the Flats** — a grinding, exploited job or a long con that wears you down
- **brine** — a bribe; money that buys silence

## Sea & boats
**Domain:** the people of the trade and their situations — crews, bosses, soldiers, marks,
rivals, meetings, passage, belonging to a family, the family itself.
Not for: goods, loot, contraband, or a score. Trade words used for trade things are not a
disguise at all — a smuggler calling contraband "cargo" is just talking shop. The words *cargo*,
*haul*, and *hold* are retired; *catch* means a person and never a score.
**Pool:** boats and their parts, moorings, knots, nets, oars, sails, gulls, fish by name, the
weather at sea, the men and women who work the boats, the hours a boat keeps.
- **a catch** — a person marked as a target (never loot)
- **the ferry** — a getaway, passage out; or crossing over (death)

## Masks & Carnival
**Domain:** identity — who someone really is, disguises, false papers, cover stories,
being recognized or unrecognized.
Not for: skills, acts, money.
**Pool:** masks, faces, paint, feathers, Carnival nights, costumes, pomanders, the maskmakers'
trade, lanterns, the parade.
- **a face / a mask** — a false identity, a disguise
- **a pomander** — a cover story that keeps trouble at arm's length

## Ivy & gardens
**Domain:** the nobility and their world only — nobles, the Duke, the Rogati, noble patronage,
poison, intrigue in the palazzi.
Not for: crime families, their patronage, or anyone below the Ivy District.
**Pool:** ivy, vines, gardens, gardeners, blooms, roots, pruning, hothouses, greenhouses,
sprigs, cuttings, the towers the ivy climbs.
- **ivy** — a noble, a patron, creeping influence
- **a sprig / something green** — poison
- **the vine** — a chain of patronage; noble intrigue

## Crows & gallows
**Domain:** death, killing, bodies, informing, denunciation, the gallows, and the price of
snitching.
Not for: ordinary danger from the Watch (that is water), or bribery (that is salt).
**Pool:** crows, ravens, rooks, feathers, roosting, the wooden platform, rope, candles, bells,
the undertakers of the House of Vornoth, the lion's mouth.
- **a crow / raven (a *corvo*)** — an informant, or an undertaker who makes bodies vanish (House of Vornoth)
- **the lion's mouth (*la bocca*)** — a drop where a written denunciation is left for the authorities
- **the wood / the platform** — the gallows; the price of snitching
- **snuffing a candle** — a killing

## The north wind
**Domain:** the Collective only — its people, its moves, its reach.
Not for: any other faction.
**Pool:** the north wind, cold, mainland weather, shutters, drafts, what the wind carries.
- **the north wind** — the Collective; faceless outsiders from the mainland
