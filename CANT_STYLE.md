# Cant style — how a term is coined

This is the **anchor** that keeps every day's term sounding like it belongs to the same city
and the same underworld. Read it every day before coining. (It is the cant's equivalent of a
conlang's phonology: the fixed rule set that makes accreted words feel related.)

## What the cant is
A **cryptolect**: ordinary Sietran words — about water, salt, fish, masks, ivy, crows, glass,
bells — reassigned to secret underworld meanings. To the Watch it sounds like dock gossip; to
those who hold the key, it's business.

## Rules for a good term
1. **Extend an existing image.** Every term must descend from a family in CORE_IMAGERY.md
   (water = law/danger, salt = money, docks & fishing = smuggling/scores, masks = identity,
   ivy = nobility/poison, crows = death/informing, the north wind = the Collective). Never
   invent an unrelated picture — that coherence is the whole point.
2. **Mundane surface.** The term is an innocent noun or short verb phrase an actual dockhand,
   fence, or salt-farmer might say. One word, or a 2–3 word phrase ("open the sluices,"
   "walk the wood," "a sprig for him").
3. **Deniable.** A full sentence using the term should still read as harmless small talk.
4. **Dry and gallows-humored.** Understated, a little grim, never melodramatic. Sietrans joke
   at the foot of the scaffold.
5. **Grounded.** Prefer Sietro specifics (the Flats, the sluices, the Fratelli piazzas, the
   north wind, the Withered Bloom) over generic thief-slang.

## What a lexicon entry looks like
Each day's entry records the **term**, its **hidden meaning**, the **image it extends**, and
one line of it **used in context**. Examples of the style (do NOT copy these — coin new ones):

- **high water** — the City Watch is out in force — *extends: tide/law* — "Keep the cart home, it's high water tonight."
- **salted** — paid off, bribed into silence — *extends: salt/money* — "The gate captain? Salted since spring."
- **a quiet face** — a forged identity that won't draw questions — *extends: mask/identity* — "She landed in the Ivy on a quiet face."
- **calling the crows** — arranging for a body to disappear — *extends: crow/death* — "That debt got settled by calling the crows."
- **the north wind's buying** — the Collective is quietly taking over an operation — *extends: north wind/Collective* — "Renoldi's old fence? The north wind's buying."

## Growth
Every 10th day, add one entry to CONVENTIONS.md — a countersign, a greeting, a rule of use, or
a district/faction variation — that fits the cant so far.
