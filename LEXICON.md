# The Cant of Sietro
- **a rook** — someone posted to watch for the Watch — *extends: crow/death* — "Put a rook on the north roof." (Day 1)
- **a light cast** — a pickpocket's theft, stealing unnoticed — *extends: docks & fishing* — "Watch that one, she's quick with a light cast." (Day 2)
- **a slack rope** — a cutpurse — *extends: docks/trade* — "Watch the slack rope near the Scambiatore." (Day 3)
- **a night haul** — a burglary, theft conducted at night — *extends: haul/score* — "That night haul cleared the collector's vault." (Day 4)
- **a borrowed face** — a housebreaker, someone who breaks into houses under false pretenses — *extends: mask/identity* — "The Ivy's had too many borrowed faces of late." (Day 5)
